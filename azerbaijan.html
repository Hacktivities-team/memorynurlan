Visa & Border Information

Visa Requirements

  • Visa Required: No (For most countries, up to 90 days)
  • Passport Validity: Must be valid for at least 6 months

Entry Process

  1. Border: Present passport
  2. Customs: Declare major currency
  3. Exit: Pass through border control

Safety and Laws

Emergency Numbers

  • General Emergency: 112
  • Police: 102
  • Emergency Medical: 103
  • Fire: 101

Local Laws

  • **Smoking:** Smoking is prohibited in most public indoor places
  • **Appropriate Dress:** Modest clothing is respectful near mosques
  • **Narcotics:** Drug possession is strictly prohibited
  • **Police Checks:** Police may request to see ID documents

Personal Safety

  • Keep copies of your documents separate from originals
  • Avoid **displaying valuable items**
  • Be cautious in less central areas at night
  • Do not leave drinks unattended

Communication

  • Get a local SIM card (e.g., Azercell, Bakcell)
  • Free WIFI is common in cafes and squares
  • Use map apps (like Google Maps/Yandex) for navigation
  • Keep emergency contacts ready

Travel Essentials

  • Always carry a form of ID/passport copy
  • Credit card payments are widespread
  • Baku Metro system is efficient
  • Use registered taxis (Bolt, Uber, Yango)

Warning: Common Scams

Taxi Scams Overcharging by non-app taxis; always use apps
Currency Exchange Use official bank branches for currency exchange
Fake Authorities If approached by police, request to see ID
Pickpocketing Be mindful in crowded areas, especially on buses/metro
